on 2026-08-16. The walk source was replaced with the corrected built-in

## Idle

Four separated standing poses: eyes open, easing closed, closed, reopening,
with subtle breathing and one stable contact line.

## Sit

Four separated seated poses: open, settle, closed, open, with a full rounded
seated belly and the thick tail curled naturally at the base.

## Walk

planted paws, visible weight transfer, restrained bob, stable back length, and
tail counterbalance. The cat must not become slimmer while walking.

The 2026-08-20 correction used the approved idle strip as exact output-scale,
head-width, cheek, neck, torso, coat, pixel, and lighting authority; the

- `sleep`: four curled deep-sleep breathing phases without kitten-like shrink.
