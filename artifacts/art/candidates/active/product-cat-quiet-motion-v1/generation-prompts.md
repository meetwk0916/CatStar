# Quiet companionship motion generation prompts

All three sources were generated with built-in ImageGen on 2026-08-09. Each
request used the approved production model sheet as the sole identity,
anatomy, face, marking, palette, and pixel-style authority; the approved v12
`sit` sheet as a production-action identity reference; and the matching older
action source for motion layout only.

## idle

Exactly four right-facing standing poses: neutral attentive stand, tiny chest
expansion with slow blink beginning, closed-eye blink, and return to neutral
with a small close-tail shift. Preserve the rounded adult body mass and avoid a
command-waiting posture.

## lie

Exactly four right-facing sphinx-like awake-rest poses: alert soft gaze, slow
blink, small relaxed head dip while awake, and return to alert rest. Keep the
head elevated, ears upright, and neutral frames open-eyed so the action remains
distinct from deep sleep.

## sleep

Exactly four right-facing curled deep-sleep poses: neutral sleep, tiny chest
expansion, restrained ear or whisker twitch, and return to neutral. Keep the
head resting down and eyes closed. A targeted edit removed unintended white
motion symbols from the initial generation without changing the cat poses.

Every request required a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background,
separated poses, stable scale and contact, no shadows or text, and no use of the
key color on the cat.
