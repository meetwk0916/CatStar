# Product Cat Prototypes v1

**Status:** Static direction approved; not runtime art

`concept-sheet-a.png` is the first refined-pixel comparison and remains as the
baseline. `concept-sheet-a-v2.png` is the current review candidate; it changes
only the anatomical separation between the first two gray-and-white
**外形原型**, ordered left to right:

1. rounded short-haired
2. slender short-haired
3. fluffy long-haired

The sheet compares enlarged seated, side-profile, and awake-rest poses, small
pixel-scale silhouettes, and in-room scale. It is a visual-direction candidate,
not a sprite sheet: poses are not frame-locked, transparent, or ready for
Phaser integration.

Compared with the baseline, v2 gives rounded short-haired a broader chest,
compact torso, shorter sturdy legs, and wider cheeks; slender short-haired gets
a narrower chest and face, longer legs and torso, larger ears, and a longer
tail. Fluffy long-haired, layout, coat, palette, lighting, and room previews are
intentionally unchanged.

## Generation

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then refined once to change
only the rendering treatment from soft pixel-like illustration to crisper,
controlled pixel clusters. The final refinement preserved the three prototype
identities, poses, gray-and-white markings, column order, room thumbnails,
lighting, and calm expressions.

The source remains internal-prototype-only and must not replace runtime art or
be used for public, paid, marketing, or app-store distribution until the
project's rights and provenance requirements are satisfied.
