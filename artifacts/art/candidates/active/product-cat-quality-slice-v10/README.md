# Product Cat Quality Slice v10

Art-directed gray-white tabby source for the Phase 0.1 cat identity gate. The
slice establishes a rounded, healthy adult silhouette and a stylized-natural
rendering language across five actions:

- `sit`: 4 frames
- `walk`: 8 frames
- `interact`: 6 frames
- `groom`: 8 frames
- `stretch`: 6 frames

The sitting, walking, and affectionate interaction rows are the agreed
three-action quality gate. Grooming and stretching extend that visual language
into the ten-action runtime contract. The remaining jump, eat, lie, and sleep
actions continue to use their previously reviewed key-pose sheets.

The transparent source sheets were generated with the built-in image generation
tool, then normalized into the CatStar `96x96` bottom-center frame contract by
`scripts/compose_product_cat_quality_slice_v10.py`.
