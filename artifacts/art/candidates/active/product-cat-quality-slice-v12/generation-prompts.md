# Quality-slice v12 generation requests

All three requests used the newly approved production model sheet as Image 1
and the corresponding v11 action source as Image 2. Image 1 was the sole
identity, anatomy, face, marking, lighting, and pixel-density authority; Image
2 was permitted to guide only motion phase ordering. Every visible cat was
redrawn from Image 1.

## Sit

Draw exactly four separated right-facing seated poses of the approved rounded
short-haired gray-and-white adult cat. Keep a stable long-dwell posture, broad
deep chest, compact substantial torso, rounded hindquarters, wide cheeks,
short neck, natural ears, amber eyes, four white-socked paws, and a medium
ringed tail resting around the body. Use restrained breathing, one slow blink,
and tiny ear/tail settling only; remain clearly seated and distinct from
standing idle. Keep identical body mass, face landmarks, marking boundaries,
warm upper-left lighting, restrained outline, controlled pixel density, scale,
and contact in all four poses.

## Walk

Draw exactly eight separated right-facing walk poses of the approved cat.
Create a complete believable domestic-cat cycle with alternating planted paws,
visible weight transfer, restrained vertical bob, stable back length, and tail
counterbalance. Preserve the approved broad deep chest, compact substantial
torso, rounded belly and hindquarters, short sturdy legs, wide cheeks, short
neck, natural ears, amber eyes, white socks, and ringed tail. The cycle must
loop without a pop or foot slide, with identical identity, marking, lighting,
outline, pixel density, scale, baseline, and contact conventions.

## Interact

Draw exactly six separated right-facing in-place poses: ordinary attentive
posture, small lean toward touch, calm slow-blink hold, gentle cheek/nuzzle
acknowledgement, release, and natural return. Acknowledge once, keep all four
paws planted, remain calm, and avoid reward-performance flourishes. Preserve
the approved broad body, short sturdy legs, wide cheeks, face landmarks,
markings, lighting, outline, pixel density, scale, baseline, and contact.

## Shared production constraints

Each request used one horizontal sheet with exactly the requested number of
non-overlapping, equally spaced poses at one scale and baseline, all facing
right, on a perfectly flat uniform `#00ff00` chroma-key background. Required
crisp stylized-natural pixel art with no floor, shadow, gradient, text, labels,
watermark, or extra objects. Avoided slender or chibi anatomy, long legs,
duplicate poses, extra limbs, floating paws, painterly blur, photographic
texture, and third-party character resemblance.
