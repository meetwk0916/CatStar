# ADR-0006: Release with one complete appearance prototype

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-07

CatStar's first release requires one coherent, production-quality rounded
short-haired **外形原型** with the complete ten-action repertoire and all ten
reviewed **毛色预设**; slender short-haired and fluffy long-haired do not block
that release. The broader three-prototype, ten-preset memorial-appearance
direction from ADR-0005 remains the product target, but shipping several
incomplete or inconsistent cats would weaken recognition and emotional trust
more than a deliberately narrow first selection. Additional prototypes become
selectable only after each receives its own complete motion master and reviewed
appearances. The first release's ten presets are complete art-reviewed
appearances—including coat pattern, eye, nose, and paw-pad colors—not
mechanical recolors. Current AI-generated candidates may guide character
design, motion breakdown, and engineering validation, but they are not release
art: the shipped ten-action master must come from one rights-cleared,
identity-consistent production process rather than incremental repair of the
current generated sheets. When cuteness conflicts with believable domestic-cat
anatomy, contact, or weight, physical credibility has veto power; warmth must
come from restrained expression, pacing, lighting, and the stylized-natural
pixel treatment instead of chibi proportions or toy-like motion. Release
acceptance must review all ten actions as continuous motion in the real room on
desktop and mobile; sprite sheets and isolated screenshots can support that
review but cannot substitute for it. `sit`, `walk`, and `interact` remain the
early moving quality slice, not a lower bar for the other seven actions: all ten
must meet the same final identity, physical-credibility, and pixel-finish
threshold. Human art review must cover the complete matrix of ten coat presets
by ten actions rather than sampling it; side-by-side loops or review boards may
batch that work, while automated frame, alpha, anchor, and dimension checks
remain supporting gates. The current generated prototype comparison locks
visual direction only. Before batch animation begins, a rights-cleared
production model sheet must lock the rounded short-haired cat's principal
views, anatomy, face landmarks, tail, marking boundaries, contact lines,
`96x96` pixel treatment, and in-room scale. The first-release repertoire is
frozen at the existing ten action classes; bespoke kneading, belly-up, chase,
zoomie, or additional play actions do not block release. Composite routines
such as approaching the user and touching the plant continue to reuse the
approved core motion.
