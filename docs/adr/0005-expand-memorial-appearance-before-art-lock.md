# ADR-0005: Expand memorial appearance before art lock

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-05

CatStar will prioritize recognition of the user's remembered cat over a single
fixed brand character. Phase 0.1 therefore targets three observable
**外形原型**—rounded short-haired, slender short-haired, and fluffy
long-haired—and ten complete, art-reviewed **毛色预设**. Each prototype must
receive its own coherent ten-action motion master before it becomes selectable;
CatStar will not stretch or reuse another prototype's body art, upload cat
photos, promise an exact replica, or expose a free-form appearance mixer. This
supersedes ADR-0003's one-prototype, six-preset Phase 0.1 limit: it accepts
higher art and QA cost because coat-only recoloring cannot provide enough
silhouette recognition for a memorial-first experience. Work remains staged:
compare all three prototypes statically in gray-and-white, complete rounded
short-haired first, then produce the other two masters and expanded presets
only after the quality workflow passes.
